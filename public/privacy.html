CRM Line — Privacy Policy

App: uz.crmline.app · Last updated: 30 July 2026

1. What the app does

The CRM Line mobile app is a mobile shell for the CRM Line platform at crmline.uz. The app does not process data on its own: it displays that web service over a secure HTTPS connection. An account issued by your company is required to use it.

2. What data is collected

The app itself does not collect, store or transmit any data from your device independently. No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs are bundled, and the Advertising ID is not used.

Any information you enter (sign-in details, work-related records) is sent directly to the crmline.uz server and processed under the terms of the CRM Line platform.

3. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Internet and network state — to load the web service and detect loss of connectivity.
  • Camera — to scan QR/bar codes and attach photos to records. The camera opens only when you start the relevant feature.

You can revoke any permission at any time in Android settings. Only the features that depend on that permission will stop working.

4. Cookies and sessions

The web service uses cookies and local storage to keep you signed in. This data is stored on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

5. Children

The app is intended for business users and is not directed to children under 13.
